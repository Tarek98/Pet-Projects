Leave and Time Off Policy

1. Annual Leave
   Full-time employees receive 15 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Unused leave may be carried over up to 5 days into the next year if approved by your manager before December 15.

2. Sick Leave
   Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave per year. For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required. Sick leave does not carry over.

3. Expense Reimbursement
   All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Travel expenses require pre-approval for trips over $500. Meals are reimbursable up to $50 per day for business travel.

4. Approval Workflows
   Leave requests over 5 consecutive days require manager approval. Expenses over $1,000 require manager and finance approval. All requests are processed within 5 business days.

5. Contact
   For policy questions, contact HR at hr@company.com or Finance at expenses@company.com.
